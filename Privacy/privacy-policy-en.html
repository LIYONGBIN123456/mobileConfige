1. Preface

This Privacy Policy applies to Cloak (hereinafter referred to as "this App"), a local app management tool built on Apple’s official frameworks including FamilyControls, DeviceActivity and ManagedSettings. It provides features such as hiding apps, scheduled locking, daily usage time limits, home screen icon disguise, group management, and full feature unlocking via VIP subscriptions.

We strictly comply with the Cybersecurity Law of the People’s Republic of China, Data Security Law, Personal Information Protection Law, Minor Online Protection Regulations, as well as global privacy standards including App Store Privacy Guidelines, GDPR and CCPA.

🔒 This App operates without proprietary cloud servers. No local user data will be uploaded online, no personally identifiable information is collected, and no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or event tracking are integrated. All business logic runs locally on your device only.

By downloading, installing, launching or using this App, you acknowledge that you have fully read, understood and voluntarily agreed to all terms of this Privacy Policy. If you disagree with any provision herein, please uninstall this App immediately and discontinue all usage.

2. Information We Will Never Collect Actively

Under no circumstances will this App actively retrieve or transmit the following data through any code, SDK or tracking logic. We never distribute, sell or rent user information to external parties:

  • Identity Information: Full name, phone number, email address, ID documents, social media accounts, residential address and other personal identifiers;
  • Payment Information: Bank card numbers, payment passwords, Apple ID credentials, payment order records;
  • Device Tracking Identifiers: IDFA, device serial numbers, IMEI and other unique hardware markers;
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates, travel trajectories;
  • User Private Content: Photos, contact lists, SMS messages, chat logs, local files;
  • Third-Party App Internal Data: Content stored within managed applications;
  • Online Behavior Records: Browsing history, search logs;
  • Diagnostic & Tracking Data: Crash reports, usage analytics, click heatmaps.

No third-party SDKs for analytics, ads, push notifications or event tracking are integrated, so there is no channel for external parties to access your local data actively or passively.

3. Necessary Data Stored Locally Only

To deliver core management functions, this App only stores configuration data within your device’s local sandbox and App Group shared containers. There is no cloud synchronization or remote backup, and all data is exclusively accessible on the current device. All stored data will be permanently erased once you uninstall the App.

1. App Management Group Configurations

  • Stored Content: Names of hidden/locked groups, anonymous app tokens you select, scheduled start & end time, weekly repeat rules, daily usage limits and group toggle statuses.
  • Purpose: Power core functions including scheduled and time-limit app hiding and locking.

2. Home Screen Disguise Settings

  • Stored Content: Alternate Icon identifiers manually selected by you.
  • Purpose: Switch the display style of this App’s home screen icon.

3. Local Screen Time Statistics

  • Stored Content: Per-app daily usage duration returned locally by Apple’s DeviceActivity API.
  • Purpose: Display native device usage stats on the homepage; data will never be transmitted externally.

4. Anonymous Subscription Entitlement Credentials

  • Stored Content: Original transaction IDs returned by Apple’s transaction system, subscription expiration dates and cycle markers.
  • Purpose: Locally verify valid VIP entitlements on your device and prevent local tampering of subscription status. This data will never be used for user tracking, profiling or shared with third parties.
📌 Special Note: This App does not store your Apple ID, full name, bank card details, shipping addresses or other identity-laden data. All billing transactions are processed independently by the official Apple App Store.

4. In-App Purchases & VIP Subscription Terms

Payments Fully Managed by Apple

All VIP subscription charges and auto-renewals are processed entirely within the App Store ecosystem. This App cannot access or interfere with payment workflows, nor does it collect or store sensitive payment data such as bank card numbers, payment passwords or Apple ID credentials.

Subscriptions Tied to Your Device

VIP benefits are bound to the current device via Apple transaction identifiers. If you reinstall the App or switch to a new device, you must restore purchases with your Apple ID. This App maintains no personal account system and cannot sync subscription status across devices.

Auto-Renewal Control

Auto-renewal settings are managed solely through your Apple account. You may disable auto-renewal anytime via: iPhone Settings > Your Apple ID > Subscriptions. No further charges will apply once auto-renewal is turned off after the current term expires.

Subscription Data Deletion

All locally saved subscription credentials will be permanently deleted upon App uninstallation and cannot be recovered.

5. Required System Permissions

All permissions listed below are mandatory to enable core management features. Disabling any corresponding permission will render related functions unavailable.

1. Screen Time Permission (FamilyControls)

Purpose: Retrieve anonymous app identifiers to enable app hiding, scheduled locking and usage limit controls. Only non-identifiable app markers are accessed; no internal content of any third-party app can be read.

2. Notification Permission

Purpose: Send alerts for activated management rules and daily usage limit reminders. You may disable this permission anytime in system Settings.

3. Alternate Icon API Permission

Purpose: Switch this App’s home screen icon via official iOS APIs after manual user selection. Only the visual icon of this App will be modified; no other applications are affected. You may revert to the default icon anytime in Settings.
