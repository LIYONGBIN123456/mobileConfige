1. Service Overview

Cloak (hereinafter referred to as "the App") is a local iOS app management tool built on Apple’s official FamilyControls, DeviceActivity and ManagedSettings frameworks. It provides local services including app hiding, scheduled locking, daily usage time limits, home screen icon disguise, group management, and full feature unlocking via VIP paid subscriptions. All control logic runs locally on your device; there is no cloud remote monitoring or remote control functionality.

The App offers Free Version and VIP Subscription Version:

  • Free Version: Limited to 1 group creation, up to 5 apps per group;
  • VIP Version: All quantity limits lifted, full features unlocked.

VIP subscriptions are processed via official App Store In-App Purchases.
